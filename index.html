The 50th Bash Itinerary: Boyd's Camp → Marathon Estate

Two iconic Keys settings in 5 days! Complete timeline and transparent guest-split excursion pricing.

🌴 The Two-Stage Experience & Lodging Coverage:
• Wed Jan 13 – Fri Jan 15: Boyd's Key West Campground (Stock Island) — Waterfront camping/glamping/cabins (guest-covered site/cabin), heated pool, Duval St & Fort Zach beach. Lester hosts & covers the Wednesday Stone Crab & Tiki Pavilion welcome gathering!
• Fri Jan 15 – Mon Jan 18: Marathon Luxury Waterfront Estate — 7-Mile Bridge transition, luxury estate compound 100% covered by Lester ($0 for guests), reef fishing, Saturday night pool BBQ bash, Sunset DJ Catamaran Cruise, and Sunset Grille farewell.
Day 1 Wednesday, Jan 13 • Key West Arrival & Boyd's Oceanfront Camp

Check-In at Boyd's Key West Campground (Stock Island) (4:00 PM)

Guest Lodging (~$175–$250 / site)

Check into waterfront RV, tent, or cabin accommodations on Stock Island. Kids and early crew splash in the heated tropical pool and stroll the marina boardwalk.

Boyd's Welcome Gathering & Accommodations: Stone Crab Feast & Oceanfront S'mores (6:00 PM)

Covered 100% by Lester ($0)

Wednesday welcome feast! Chilled Florida stone crab claws, Key West pink shrimp, craft cocktails at Boyd's waterfront tiki pavilion, followed by roasting s'mores over oceanfront fire pits under the palm trees — 100% hosted & covered by Lester ($0 for guests).

Casual Dockside Dinner at Hogfish Bar & Grill (8:00 PM)

Guest-Split (~$40–$55 / adult)

World-famous fresh hogfish sandwiches, conch fritters, and open-air harbor breezes right on Safe Harbor marina docks.

Day 2 Thursday, Jan 14 • Fort Zachary Taylor Beach & Mallory Square Sunset

Fort Zachary Taylor State Park Beach & Snorkel (10:00 AM – 1:00 PM)

Park Entry ~$2.50 / person (~$7 / vehicle)

Key West's best beach where the Atlantic meets the Gulf. Calm turquoise water for snorkeling, shaded pine groves, sandcastles, and historic brick fort exploration.

Historic Seaport Lunch at Half Shell Raw Bar (1:30 PM)

Guest-Split (~$30–$45 / adult)

Iconic open-air dockside seafood shack with fresh Gulf oysters on the half shell, peel-and-eat shrimp, and cold Key West ale.

Old Town / Duval St Stroll & Mallory Square Sunset (3:30 PM – 6:30 PM)

Free Public Celebration ($0)

Explore legendary Duval Street, enjoy Cuban coffee and key lime pie on a stick, then witness Mallory Square's world-famous sunset celebration with street performers and fire-dancers.

Waterfront Dinner at The Perry Hotel / Matt's Stock Island (7:30 PM)

Guest-Split (~$45–$65 / adult)

Harbor-view dining along Charter Row marina, followed by evening chill time around Boyd's waterfront campfire.

Day 3 Friday, Jan 15 • Bahia Honda Beach, Keys Fishery & Marathon Estate Check-In

Pack Up Boyd's & Bahia Honda State Park Beach Stop (10:30 AM – 1:00 PM)

Park Entry ~$2.50 / person (~$9 / vehicle)

Cross onto Calusa & Sandspur beaches at Bahia Honda — ultra-shallow, crystal-clear calm shallows perfect for kids (ages 3–7) to wade safely, plus breathtaking Seven Mile Bridge panoramas.

Dockside Lunch at Keys Fishery Upstairs Raw Bar (1:15 PM – 2:30 PM)

Guest-Split (~$30–$45 / adult)

World-famous original Lobster Reubens, stone crab claws, and smoked fish dip on the open-air marina deck in Marathon.

Check-In at Marathon Waterfront Luxury Estate (3:00 PM – 5:30 PM)

Covered 100% by Lester ($0)

Transition into the private 6–8 bedroom luxury waterfront compound as the full weekend crew arrives. Master suite room assignments, dockside unpacking, and poolside cold drinks.

Friday Sunset Arrival Poolside Chill & Refreshments (6:30 PM)

Covered by Lester ($0)

Casual estate arrival bites, charcuterie, cold craft beers, tropical cocktails, and evening pool swim under the lights as everyone settles in.

Day 4 Saturday, Jan 16 • Reef Fishing, Sombrero Beach & Lester's 50th Sunset DJ Cruise

Morning Option A: Private Reef & Offshore Sportfishing Charter (8:00 AM – 12:00 PM)

$235 / angler (Split 6 ways, all gear & tips included)

Sweet E'Nuf or Main Attraction private charter targeting yellowtail snapper, kingfish, and blackfin tuna. Catch will be prepped for fresh ceviche and dinner!

Morning Option B: Sombrero Beach Relax & Playground (9:30 AM – 12:00 PM)

100% Free Public Beach ($0)

Non-anglers and kids relax at Marathon's finest white sand beach — barrier-reef protected calm shallows, shaded tiki pavilions, playground, and oceanfront breeze.

The Main 50th Event: Private Sunset DJ Catamaran Cruise (5:30 PM – 9:30 PM)

Guest-Split (~$230–$480 / adult; Kids free)

The centerpiece of Lester's 50th! Private 50–65ft luxury sailing catamaran, live South Florida event DJ, open bar (champagne, beer, wine, cocktails), passed shrimp & conch appetizers, and dancing under open ocean skies with zero noise curfews!

Saturday Night 50th Birthday BBQ & Pool Bash (6:30 PM)

Covered 100% by Lester ($0)

Lester's signature birthday BBQ feast by the estate heated pool! Grilled skirt steak with chimichurri, Key West pink shrimp skewers, craft beers, signature cocktails, and poolside celebration under the lights.

Day 5 Sunday, Jan 17 • Sombrero Beach Morning, Sandbar Flotilla & Sunset Grille Farewell

Morning Lanai Breakfast & Sombrero Beach Coastal Walk (9:30 AM – 12:00 PM)

100% Free ($0)

Avocado toast, tropical fruit, espresso on the lanai, followed by beach walks and shell collecting with the kids at Sombrero Beach.

Backcountry Sandbar Boat Excursion: Snipe Point / Mud Keys (1:00 PM – 4:30 PM)

Guest-Split ($120 / adult, $240 / couple; Kids free)

Private boat cruise to waist-deep turquoise flats. Giant floating lily pad mats, paddleboards, cold drink coolers, and sun-soaked relaxation.

Farewell Golden Hour Dinner at Sunset Grille & Raw Bar (6:00 PM)

Guest-Split (~$55–$75 / adult)

Celebratory final group dinner right at the foot of the historic Seven Mile Bridge. Multi-level open-air tiki decks, oceanfront pool, fresh stone crab, Mahi-Mahi, sushi, and panoramic sunset views.

Day 6 Monday, Jan 18 (MLK Day) • Departures & Farewell to Paradise

Cuban Coffee, Pastelitos & Estate Check-Out (10:00 AM)

Included ($0)

Warm guava pastelitos, cafe con leche, group photos on the dock, packing up, and check-out by 10:00 AM.

Airport Flights & Scenic Drive Home (11:00 AM)

—

Departures via Key West Airport (EYW) or scenic Overseas Highway drive back to Miami (MIA) / Fort Lauderdale (FLL).
